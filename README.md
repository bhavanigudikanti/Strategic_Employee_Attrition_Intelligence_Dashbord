Strategic Employee Attrition Intelligence Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The Strategic Employee Attrition Intelligence Dashboard is a Power BI-based data analytics project designed to analyze employee attrition and workforce characteristics.

The project uses employee data to identify patterns related to attrition, department, job role, work mode, salary growth, overtime, burnout, performance, career level, training, and employee retention.

Microsoft Power BI is used for data preprocessing, analysis, visualization, KPI creation, and interactive dashboard development.

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the overall employee attrition rate.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition.
- Analyze the relationship between work mode and attrition.
- Study the impact of overtime, burnout, performance, and salary growth.
- Analyze employee retention based on education, career level, tenure, and marital status.
- Provide interactive dashboards to support HR analysis and decision-making.

📊 Dataset

The dataset contains employee-related information such as:

- Department
- Job Role
- Work Mode
- Job Level
- Overtime
- Burnout
- Performance
- Salary Growth
- Education Level
- Career Information
- Attrition Status
- Employee Engagement
- Manager Support
- Years at Company

The dataset contains 5,000 employees, of which 895 employees have left, giving an overall attrition rate of approximately 18%.
